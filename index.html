Pharmacy Details & Settings

📁 Data folder

Pharmamitra keeps everything in one folder: Data (live copy of your data), Bills (a copy of every bill), Backups (one backup file per day) and Excel (exports). Works in Chrome / Edge on a computer.

☁ Google Drive sync

Paste the Google Client ID once, then press Connect Google at the top. After that every change is copied to your own Google Drive (a private hidden app folder). On a new device press Restore from Google Drive on the login screen.
